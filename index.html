The Pit on Main

Low heat.
Long hours.
No shortcuts.

McDonald BBQ has held down the bottom of Main Street long enough that folks plan their week around it. The ribs go on low and slow, the greens simmer down with smoke and time, and the chops get smothered the way somebody's grandmother taught somebody's grandmother.

It is a cash kitchen and a takeout window, plain and honest. You order, you wait the good kind of wait, and you carry home a foil tray that feeds the whole table. In a neighborhood of Orange that runs on Caribbean, Southern, and island roots, this is the smell everybody recognizes.
